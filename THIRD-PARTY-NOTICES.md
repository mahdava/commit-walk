## SuperTrend: the lettering of the logo

The lettering in `assets/commit-walk-logo.svg`,
`assets/commit-walk-logo-outlined.svg` and `assets/keyboard.svg` is set in
[SuperTrend](https://nhfonts.gumroad.com/l/super-trend-font) by
[NHFonts](https://nhfonts.gumroad.com/), a free font for commercial use under
the EULA that comes with it. Only the letter shapes, as vector outlines, are
in this repository; the font itself is not.
